How it works

1

Set your profile

Enter your age, weight, height, sex, and activity level. Your TDEE is calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

2

Pick a goal & macro split

Choose Cut (−20%), Maintain, or Bulk (+15%). Then set your carb / protein / fat ratio using a preset or the sliders.

3

Log food to a meal

Pick a target meal, then search the database and hit Add. Adjust portion size with the × multiplier or the +/− stepper on each logged item.

4

Track your progress

The ring and macro bars update in real time. Your data is saved locally in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
